## Purpose

This app is to give you a way to calculate catch and flee rates in real time for Safari Zone Battles across Pokemon Generations. The goal is to allow you to enter the generation you're playing, choose the encounter you have on screen, then select the step you're going to take and have it show you what the catch and flee rates will be once you make your move.

### Currently Supported Games

❌ Red/Blue/Yellow  
❌ Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald  
✅ FireRed/LeafGreen

## Install/Update/Uninstall

Install: `curl -s https://github.com/melvinquick/safari_battle_tracker/raw/branch/main/install.py | python3 -`
